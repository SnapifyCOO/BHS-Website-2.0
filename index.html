WHO WE ARE

My passion for entrepreneurship started young with a DJ side hustle in middle school. In 2019, my family decided to open a food truck, and we dove in headfirst. We loved cooking great food and serving our community, but like many small business owners, we faced long days and very little clarity on whether all that work was truly paying off.

We were busy every day… but our bank account didn't reflect the effort.

That frustration and love of the business pushed me to pursue a degree in Agribusiness at Virginia State University, where I graduated in 2024. During the summer of 2023, I had the opportunity to apply what I was learning directly to our family business.

The first step was simple but powerful: organize the financials. Once I had clean, updated monthly data, the problems became visible. Repair and maintenance costs were high because we were too overworked to properly care for our equipment. Fuel expenses were elevated because we were traveling to multiple venues daily without optimized routes.

I began analyzing performance data by location and day of the week. The numbers revealed something eye-opening! Some venues were not profitable on certain days. By identifying our strongest locations by day and restructuring our weekly schedule around that data, we reduced unnecessary travel, lowered fuel costs, improved routing efficiency, and decreased labor hours. The improved schedule also gave us the time to stay ahead on preventative maintenance, reducing long-term repair costs.

With accurate monthly reporting in place, we could consistently monitor performance, maintain cost efficiency, and keep everyone aligned. I also conducted operational "stress tests" to evaluate new ideas before implementation, helping us improve efficiency while minimizing risk.

The results were transformational.

D & M Jamaican Kitchen went from barely breaking even in 2022 to clearing $41,995 in profit in 2023. In 2024, we further shifted our strategy to focus exclusively on high-revenue, high-margin events and cleared $32,821 profit in just three months. Not only were we making money, but my family finally had more time to themselves.

That process ignited something in me.

I realized the biggest thing holding small businesses back isn't effort, it's clarity. Most owners are working hard, but they don't truly know their numbers. Without financial clarity, it's nearly impossible to identify inefficiencies, create strong plans, or build sustainable growth.

So in 2024, I launched TheBusinessHandyMan LLC to help other small business owners experience the same breakthrough my family did.

Today, TheBusinessHandyMan LLC helps entrepreneurs create financial clarity, streamline operations, and turn scattered effort into structured growth so they can build profitable businesses with less stress and more freedom.
